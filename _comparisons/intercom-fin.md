Intercom Fin and similar agents are built for one job: answer customers instantly from your help center, your macros and your past conversations. When the answer is written down somewhere, they are very good at it.

The trouble starts when the answer is not written down. "I was charged twice", "my export is empty", "the invoice shows the wrong VAT number". Those are not documentation questions. Someone has to look at the account, the data and the code. Today, that someone is a developer.

## Side by side

| | Intercom Fin | Deflekt |
|---|---|---|
| Answers from | Help center, macros, past replies | Your code, production data, error tracker, customer history |
| Typical outcome | Conversation closed with an article | Root cause, reply, console command or tested fix |
| Bugs | Escalated to a human | Investigated, fixed in a pull request |
| Customers who did not write | Not visible | Found in the data and contacted proactively |
| Who uses it | Customers, in the chat | Your support and engineering team |
| Acts on its own | Replies to customers directly | Never, every action waits for approval |

## When to use which

**Use Fin** when most of your volume is "how do I" questions that your documentation already answers. It will deflect them before a human sees them.

**Use Deflekt** when the expensive part of your queue is the rest: the tickets support cannot close without engineering. That is where developer hours go, and where Fin hands over.

## Together

The two fit well. Fin closes what is documented, and everything it escalates goes to Deflekt, which investigates it in the code and the data and hands your team a ready answer. Your customers get instant replies to simple questions, and real answers to hard ones. Deflekt connects to [Intercom](/integrations/intercom/), [Zendesk](/integrations/zendesk/) and [Freshdesk](/integrations/freshdesk/).
