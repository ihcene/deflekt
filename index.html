§ 1 · The maintenance tax

Shipping is half the job. The other half lands in your inbox.

Most of a product's cost comes after launch. It arrives from four directions, and today every one of them ends up on a developer's desk.

i.

Customer tickets

In every language, with screenshots, often about something that broke for a single account.The export is empty again.

ii.

Errors and alerts

Stack traces with no customer attached, piling up until someone finds the time to look.NoMethodError, 340 events.

iii.

Silent data bugs

A feature stops writing, a column goes empty. No error, no ticket, until a customer notices weeks later.Nobody reports what nobody sees.

iv.

Questions and requests

Sales, support and ops asking how the product behaves, or for a small change, in a Slack thread.Do we already send a reminder before the due date?

Each one costs a developer an hour of context switching. Deflekt takes the hour.

§ 9 · Questions
How is this different from Cursor, Claude Code or Devin?

Those tools help a developer who already sat down to build something. Deflekt works before that moment: it receives requests from people who don't code, investigates them in your code and data, and decides whether the answer is a reply, a command or a change. When code is needed it can use the same agents, and it adds what they lack: customer context, production data, proof and memory.

How is it different from AI support agents like Intercom Fin?

Support agents answer from your help center and close conversations. Deflekt goes into the code and the data to find the real cause, and fixes bugs instead of explaining them. It works alongside your support team and your help desk.

Does it change anything in production on its own?

No. Deflekt reads production, it never writes to it. Fixes go through pull requests, your tests and your review. Console commands are prepared for a human to run.

Where does my code run?

Workers that read code, run tests and record QA run on your infrastructure. The hub only stores analyses and results. A fully self hosted setup is available.

Which languages and stacks does it support?

Any language your customers write in, and any stack your engineers work in. It runs daily on Rails, React and React Native codebases, with tickets in Dutch, French and English.

How much does it cost?

We are onboarding a small group of design partners: we set Deflekt up on your stack and tune it with you. Pricing depends on the size of your team and is discussed on the first call.
