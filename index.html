What it does

Analysis

--analyze
AI reads every ticket, understands the issue, checks your codebase, and queries production data. In any language.
--attachments
Analyzes images, PDFs, CSVs, and logs attached to tickets using vision AI. No manual download needed.
--query
Runs production database lookups to verify root causes and pull real customer data into the analysis.
--sources
Ingests from Freshdesk, Sentry, JIRA, and more into a single prioritized queue.

Automation

--autofix
Finds the bug in your code, writes the fix, runs your tests, and opens a draft PR. You just review the diff.
--reply
Drafts multilingual customer replies in your tone and voice. High-confidence replies can be sent with one click.

Intelligence

--briefing
Daily patterns across all tickets. What is breaking, what is urgent, what you can batch-close.
--memory
Harvests institutional knowledge from every resolved ticket. Builds a searchable knowledge base that gets smarter over time.

Visibility

--dashboard
Web UI to filter, review, and act on your entire support queue. Priorities, categories, and status at a glance.
--costs
Tracks AI spend per ticket, per attachment, and per week. Know exactly what triage costs before your bill arrives.
